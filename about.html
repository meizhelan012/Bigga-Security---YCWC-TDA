What is Digital Security?

Digital security is the practice of protecting information, data, and systems from digital threats such as hacking, data theft, and cyberattacks. It involves using technology, policies, and measures to maintain the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information in the digital world.

Why It Matters

In today's digital age, digital security is essential because many aspects of our lives depend on technology. Cyber threats can cause financial loss, privacy violations, and even risks to physical safety. By understanding and applying good digital security practices, we can protect ourselves and our information from increasingly complex threats in the digital world.
