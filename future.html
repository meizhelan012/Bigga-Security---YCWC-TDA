Future Application Vision

As technology continues to advance, its risks, innovations, and security challenges will evolve as well. These developments can bring both benefits and drawbacks. We must also anticipate the potential risks through the concepts below:

AI Security

As AI technology develops, AI security must also receive attention. AI could be used for harmful purposes, such as creating deepfakes or even developing more sophisticated viruses. Therefore, we must anticipate these risks by creating stronger security systems for AI.

Personal Data Security

In today's digital age, personal data security is becoming increasingly important. As more online services require personal information, data protection is a shared responsibility. We must remain alert to the risk of data breaches and take preventive measures to keep our information confidential.
